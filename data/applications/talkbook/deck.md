## Sales calls disappear into thin air

**Reps hate CRM entry**

Reps spend about 5 hours a week on notes and CRM updates.

**Managers cannot coach**

Managers hear about 3% of their team's calls.

**Indian languages break tools**

Hindi-English and Tamil- English calls are poorly transcribed by global tools.
