# Pumpwise

Predictive maintenance for small-factory motors and pumps

Pre-seed | Raising INR 2.2 Cr | Pune

---

## A pump failure stops the whole line

**Breakdowns halt production**

A failed pump at a small plant stops work for 1 to 3 days.

**Monitoring costs too much**

Imported condition monitors cost INR 40,000+ per motor, with annual contracts.

**Checks are by hand**

Technicians do weekly rounds, so failures are found after they happen.

From visits to 38 plants in the Pune and Coimbatore clusters, Nov 2025 to Feb 2026.

## Why now

**Cheap sensors**

Vibration and temperature chips now cost under INR 1,200 a unit.

**Cheap connectivity**

Low-power radios and Indian mobile data make remote monitoring affordable.

**Small-data models**

A model can learn a motor's normal pattern in 2 weeks, with no failure history.

## Clip on, learn, alert

**1. Clip on**

Fit a sensor on the motor in 10 minutes. No wiring.

**2. Learn**

The model learns normal vibration and heat over 14 days.

**3. Alert**

WhatsApp alert when behaviour drifts, days before failure.
