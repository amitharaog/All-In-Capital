# CareCorner

Neighbourhood clinics with diagnostics for Tier-2 India

Pre-seed | Raising INR 3 Cr | Hyderabad

---

## Tier-2 families lack good primary care

**Hospitals are far**

Families travel 20 to 40 km for a basic consultation and tests.

**Quality varies**

Small clinics often lack tests and follow-up.

**Costs surprise**

Families pay per visit with no continuity of care.

_CareCorner 3_

## Consult, test, follow up

**1. Consult**

A family doctor in a 1,200 sq ft neighbourhood clinic.

**2. Test**

In-house diagnostics with results the same day.

**3. Follow up**

WhatsApp reminders and a health record per family.

## Patient acquisition and clinic economics

| Channel | Share of new patients |
|---|---|
| Local doctor referrals | 45% of new patients |
| WhatsApp and Google listings | 35% of new patients |
| Health camps and tie-ups | 20% of new patients |

- **INR 210** cost per new patient
- **INR 28 L** capex per clinic
- **Month 8** breakeven

Revenue per clinic at maturity: INR 1.4 Cr a year, 62% from consultations and diagnostics, 38% from pharmacy.
