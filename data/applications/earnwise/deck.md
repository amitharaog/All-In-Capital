## Workers wait a month for pay they have earned

**Cash gaps**

Gig and shift workers face expenses long before payday.

**Expensive loans**

Many borrow at 5 to 10% a month from informal lenders.

**Attrition**

Employers lose workers who need money sooner.

## Earn, withdraw, repay

**1. Earn**

Workers see wages they have already earned.

**2. Withdraw**

They withdraw up to 50% instantly, for a flat fee.

**3. Repay**

The employer deducts it from the next payroll.
