# RentRoute

Pay your rent with your credit card

Pre-seed | Raising INR 1.8 Cr | Bengaluru

---

## Rent is the biggest bill

**Biggest expense**

Rent takes 25 to 40% of a young professional's income.

**Cards sit idle**

Landlords will not accept cards, so reward points go unused.

**Timing gap**

Rent is due on the 1st. Salary may arrive later.

## Add, pay, done

**1. Add rent details**

Enter your landlord's bank details once.

**2. Pay by card**

Pay rent with any credit card.

**3. Landlord gets cash**

Rent reaches the landlord's bank account within a day.
