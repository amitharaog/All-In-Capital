## SMEs wait 60 days to get paid

**Late payments**

SMEs wait an average 60 days to receive payment from buyers.

**Costly credit**

Informal invoice discounting costs 24 to 36% a year.

**Manual collections**

Teams chase buyers on calls and spreadsheets.

## Invoice, collect, finance

**1. Invoice**

Send invoices with a payment link.

**2. Collect**

Buyers pay by UPI, card or bank transfer.

**3. Finance**

Get paid early against any invoice, at 1.2% a month.
